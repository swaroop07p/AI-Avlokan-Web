About AI AVLOKAN 2k26

AI AVLOKAN 2K26 is the Flagship National-Level AI Immersion Fest, presented by the AIML Department of JNNCE, Shivamogga. It brings together students, innovators, and AI enthusiasts to explore the exciting world of Artificial Intelligence and emerging technologies. Through engaging challenges, quizzes, creative competitions, and hands-on experiences, participants put their skills and ideas to the test. The fest is designed to foster innovation, collaboration, creativity, and problem-solving in a dynamic AI-driven environment.

About the Department — AIML

The Department of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AIML) at JNNCE, Shivamogga is committed to nurturing innovative thinkers and future AI professionals. The department provides strong foundations in AI, Machine Learning, Data Science, Deep Learning, and emerging technologies. It encourages students to transform ideas into creative solutions for real-world challenges.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI Avlokan 2k26?

AI Avlokan 2k26 is a national-level technical festival that brings together students from colleges and universities to participate in technical competitions, challenges, and other exciting events.

Is there a registration fee?

Yes, there is a nominal base registration fee. Check for the Fee Structure which is provided.

Can I participate in multiple events?

Yes, as long as the event timings do not overlap. Check the schedule before registering.

Is accommodation provided for outstation participants?

Accommodation can be arranged on prior request at a subsidized cost. Please inform the Organizers before.

Who can participate in the fest?

Students from colleges, universities, and technical institutions can participate.

Do I need a college ID card?

Yes participants are required to present a valid college/institution ID card for verification. It is recommended that you carry your ID card throughout the fest.

Will food be provided during the fest?

Yes. Breakfast and lunch will be provided to all registered participants, and the cost is included in the registration fee.

Will participants receive certificates for the technical fest?

Yes! All registered participants will receive e-certificates for their participation in the fest. In addition, winners of the respective events will receive special winning certificates recognizing their achievement and performance.

What if I have a question that is not answered in the FAQ?

You can contact the organizing team through the official contact information provided on the website. Our coordinators will be happy to assist you.
